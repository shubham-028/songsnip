Play the song. Press [ where a part you like starts and ] where it ends. Repeat that for every part you want. SongSnip plays all your parts one after another as a single track, blending each into the next, and skips everything in between. Use ↑ ↓ to change the order, for example to put the chorus first. To fine-tune a part, drag its green handles or type times such as 1:23.5. If you don't mark any parts, the whole song plays.

For seamless joins: while the song plays, press T on every beat for a few seconds, starting on a "1". Bar lines then appear on the timeline. Click ✨ Make perfect joins to move every part onto the bar lines, so the beat carries on smoothly into the next part and into the next song. While snapping is on, new parts and dragged handles snap to the bar lines too. Hold Alt while dragging to place a handle freely.
